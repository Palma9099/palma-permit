How Palma resolves unpermitted work & alterations in Melbourne

The City of Melbourne permits work within city limits through its own building department, while unincorporated Brevard County permits through its Planning and Development Department under Chapter 22 (Buildings and Building Regulations) of the county code and the Florida Building Code; the county uses the Permit Assist platform and the BASS database for existing-permit lookups. Under Florida Statute 553.791, a licensed private provider can perform plan review and inspections in place of staff, which can shorten timelines on a stalled record.

This is a Space Coast market, so Atlantic barrier islands and Indian River Lagoon frontage sit in FEMA Special Flood Hazard Areas and the wind-borne debris region, making base-flood elevation, opening protection, and product approvals frequent review triggers. Coastal storm repairs are where open permits, expired permits, and unpermitted work most often surface here, and where Palma's coordination gets a case to a clean closeout.
