The kinds of problems we resolve

Representative situations, not specific clients — these are the everyday scenarios Palma is built to handle.

The open permit found at closing

A title search turns up a roof or A/C permit from years ago that was never finalized, and the sale is days away. Palma pulls the record, identifies the missing inspections, and coordinates the closeout so the deal can move forward.

The violation racking up fines

An owner gets a code-enforcement notice for unpermitted work with daily fines accruing. Palma maps the corrections, handles the after-the-fact permitting through licensed pros, and pursues case closure and fine mitigation.

The submittal that keeps getting rejected

A contractor's package bounces at the counter for missing product approvals or HVHZ details. Palma's partners prepare a complete, sealed set built to the jurisdiction's exact checklist so it clears review.
