Coral Gables Final Inspection & Sign-Off FAQ

Can you help me get a certificate of occupancy in Coral Gables?

Yes. We coordinate the remaining inspections, clear outstanding items, and drive the permit to final sign-off and a certificate of occupancy or completion in Miami-Dade County.

What is a private provider inspection?

Under Florida Statute 553.791, licensed private providers can perform inspections in place of the local building department, often faster. Palma coordinates these for your Coral Gables project.

Does Palma perform the inspections itself?

No. Palma coordinates independently licensed Florida inspectors and private providers. Palma is a permitting and project-coordination company, not an inspection firm.
