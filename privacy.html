What we collect

Free checklist-mode checks run entirely in your browser — no documents are uploaded. For automated deep-reads, the files you upload are processed only to generate your readiness report. We also collect basic account information (name, email, organization) and standard usage analytics.

How we use it

To provide and improve the service, generate your reports, support your account, and communicate about your plan. We do not sell your data or your uploaded documents.

Document handling & security

Uploaded files are processed to produce your report and are retained only as needed for your project history. You can delete uploaded files from your project history at any time. We use encryption in transit and at rest and operate on SOC 2-aligned infrastructure.

Third parties

We use reputable infrastructure and processing providers under data-processing agreements. Enterprise customers can request a current sub-processor list and a DPA.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.
