Drawings & Engineering FAQ

Do you provide sealed, signed engineering drawings?

Yes. Drawings and structural calculations are prepared and sealed by independently licensed Florida engineering partners, built to pass jurisdiction review.

Can you handle HVHZ and flood-zone requirements?

Yes. We handle High-Velocity Hurricane Zone requirements, Miami-Dade NOA / Florida product approvals, flood elevation, and freeboard rules as part of the package.

What do I need to get a drawing started?

An address and a description of the work. For a quick read on cost, timeline, and code flags, run the Permit Ready feasibility report first.

Is Palma an engineering firm?

No. Palma Building Solutions is a permitting and project-coordination company. Engineering is performed and sealed by a vetted network of independently licensed Florida professionals.
