Local note — Big Pine Key. Big Pine Key is unincorporated Lower Keys, permitted by the Monroe County Building Department, and parts fall within protected Key deer habitat that can add environmental review. The area is almost entirely in FEMA flood zones where elevation and the 50% substantial-improvement rule apply, and Lower Keys design wind speeds are among the highest in the state.
