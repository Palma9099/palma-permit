About

Built by people who pull permits

Palma Permit is a product of Palma LLC, a Florida company working at the intersection of construction, engineering, and software.

Every week, solid projects stall in plan review for preventable reasons - a missing form, an outdated code reference, a county-specific requirement nobody outside that county has heard of. The knowledge to avoid those rejections exists, but it lives in scattered checklists and the heads of a few senior people.

Palma Permit turns that knowledge into software: jurisdiction profiles, automated package checks, and a deep-read engine that reviews plans the way a seasoned permit tech would - before the building department does.
